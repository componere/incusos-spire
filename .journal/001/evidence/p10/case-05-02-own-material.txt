  Status alone is not the proof, exactly as the plan warns. The 503 is a POST-consumption
  burn, which means the broker had already resolved the binding before issuance was
  attempted; the log line names that resolved binding as instance_uuid=a4fec3ea-... —
  B — and the 'bootstrap key cleared' line names instance_name=spike-guest-b, i.e. the
  broker went back to B's own config key to clear it. The failure is 'no entry matched
  B's selectors', not 'A's identity was refused at the last moment'. B's own material
  bound to B on the authoritative path, and A's UUID never entered the resolution at all.
