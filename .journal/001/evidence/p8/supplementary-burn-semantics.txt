=== supplementary measurement — does a REJECTED redemption burn the nonce?
date=2026-08-17T04:03:38Z
Why: internal/nonce Redeem calls the atomic ConsumeOnce (which commits Used=true) and only
then compares the consumed record against the live instance. A generation change should
therefore consume the nonce, while a failure raised BEFORE the consume (case e's
'attestor: instance not found') should leave it unconsumed. That difference decides whether
a guest may safely retry after a transient rejection. It is measured here, not asserted.
