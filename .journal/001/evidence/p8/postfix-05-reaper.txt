No redemption of b05987fa… appears anywhere in the log, because none happened.
The only actor was the sweep, and the withdrawal names the nonce ID and the
instance UUID so an operator can tell a reaped nonce from a redeemed one. The
grace window is visible as designed: the key survived expiry by roughly ten
seconds and then went, which is what keeps an expired nonce answering 409 rather
than 401 for callers who are merely late.

Finding F2 is closed in code. It is worth being precise about what "closed"
means: the value stops working at expires_at, and the reaper bounds how long the
dead value lingers in instance configuration. It does not shorten the window in
which a live nonce is a bearer credential — that is the untouched architectural
finding SEC-009, recorded in SECURITY_FINDINGS.md.
